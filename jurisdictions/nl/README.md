# Netherlands jurisdiction pack

The NL pack overlays EU/EEA authority with Dutch regulator procedures, terminology and official government methodologies.

Initial references:

- Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens guidance and notification procedures;
- Dutch Government DPIA/pre-scan methodology and information model;
- Dutch-language EDPB materials where available.

## Intended outputs

- NL-specific breach notification adapter;
- Dutch DPIA/pre-scan adapter;
- Dutch terminology and report labels;
- NL-specific synthetic evaluation cases.

The pack must not turn government methodology fields into legal obligations unless a binding source independently supports that classification.
