# EU/EEA jurisdiction pack

Initial legal authority set:

- GDPR via EUR-Lex;
- EDPB guidance and decisions;
- EU AI Act where the workflow scope requires it;
- European Commission official SCCs where contracts/transfers are in scope.

This pack should define EU-wide legal propositions before national overlays are applied.

## Rules

- binding law outranks guidance;
- consultation drafts are never promoted to binding requirements;
- source version and verification date must be attached to material legal conclusions;
- national guidance may supplement but not silently replace EU authority.
